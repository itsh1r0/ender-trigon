Small release for **Ender Trigon**, adding tamable baby ender dragons, a config, and more.

Now dependent on [CrackersLib](https://github.com/nonamecrackers2/crackerslib)

**Additions/Tweaks**

- Made the time it takes for the Ender Dragon to perch take less time during its "landing approach" phase (configurable)
- Added a config, with the following options:
   - Configs to disable certain custom attacks
   - A skip landing phase config option
   - Ability to change the maximum amount of baby ender dragons that can be spawned by the Ender Dragon during its "dive bomb" phase
   - Ability to toggle block flinging during the "crash" phase
   - Ability to change the amount of attacks the Ender Dragon must do until it can perch
- Made the baby ender dragons tamable
   - There's a chance for baby ender dragon eggs to appear on the ground randomly during the Ender Dragon fight
   - Place one down and let it hatch
   - Will attack most typical hostile mobs (except creepers) for the player
   - Will attack anything the player attacks or gets hurt by
- Added the dragon horn item, which can be used to call tamed baby ender dragons to sit
	- Can also be used to make them fly again
	- Requires four eyes of ender around a goat horn to craft
- Made the Ender Dragon require, by default, 4 special attacks to be done before it perches instead of 5
